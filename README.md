# AI Job Search Guide

A beginner-friendly manual and static site for using AI in your job search — resumes, cover letters, interview practice, application tracking, and what never to paste online.

Written in plain English. No experience needed.

## Who this is for

Anyone looking for work who wants to use AI as a helper, not a replacement. Whether you're writing your first resume or changing careers after years in one field, this guide walks you through each step slowly and clearly.

This guide is published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — an Indiana LLC focused on beginner-friendly AI education, books, guides, and practical tools. Learn more at dylanasdale.github.io.

## What's inside

- `docs/` — the full manual in Markdown, one chapter per topic
- `site/` — a static HTML version of the same manual, plus a private application tracker that runs entirely in your browser
- `LICENSE` — MIT license, Copyright (c) 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC

## Chapters

1. Getting Started — what AI is and how to talk to it
2. Resumes — drafting and tailoring with AI
3. Cover Letters — writing letters that sound like you
4. Interview Practice — practicing questions and answers with AI
5. Application Tracking — keeping track of where you applied
6. Privacy & Safety — what never to paste online
7. Cheat Sheet — ready-to-copy prompts

## How to use the HTML site

Open `site/index.html` in any web browser. Nothing needs to be installed, and nothing is sent over the internet. The application tracker (`site/tracker.html`) saves only in your browser's local storage — your data never leaves your computer.

## How to use the Markdown docs

Read the files in `docs/` with any text editor, or view them on GitHub. Each chapter stands on its own, so you can jump to the part you need right now.

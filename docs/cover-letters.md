# Cover Letters — Writing Letters That Sound Like You

## What a cover letter is for

A cover letter is a short letter that goes with your resume. It explains why you want this job and why you are a good fit. It is your chance to sound like a real person, not a list of bullet points.

A good cover letter has four parts:

1. **Header** — your contact info and the date
2. **Opening** — the job you are applying for and why you are excited
3. **Body** — 1–2 paragraphs connecting your experience to the job
4. **Closing** — a thank-you and a request for an interview

Keep it to one page, three or four short paragraphs.

## Step 1: Gather what you need

- The job title and company name
- The hiring manager's name (if you can find it; "Dear Hiring Manager" works fine otherwise)
- 2–3 things from the job posting that match your experience
- A short story about a time you helped someone or solved a problem at work

## Step 2: Ask AI to draft a cover letter

Copy this prompt and fill in the brackets:

> You are a friendly writing coach. Write a one-page cover letter in plain English, warm but professional.
>
> Job: [Job Title] at [Company]
> Hiring manager: [Name or "Dear Hiring Manager"]
> My experience: [2–3 sentences about your background]
> Why I want this job: [1–2 sentences]
> A story about my work: [short example, 2–3 sentences]
>
> Structure: opening, one body paragraph connecting my experience to the job, one short story paragraph, and a closing asking for an interview. Use plain English. Do not sound robotic. Do not include my real address — use [City, State].

## Step 3: Make it sound like you

Read the draft out loud. If a sentence feels like something you would never say, rewrite it in your own words. Employers can tell when a letter is copied word-for-word from AI. Use AI for the structure, then make the words your own.

## Step 4: Check the details

- Did you spell the company name correctly?
- Did you mention the right job title?
- Is your email and phone correct?
- Did you leave in any placeholders like [Company] or [Your Name]?

# Interview Practice — Practicing Questions and Answers with AI

## Why practice with AI

AI is a safe place to practice interview answers because it does not judge you, it never gets tired, and you can try the same answer ten different ways. It is especially helpful if you are nervous, out of practice, or changing careers.

AI is not a substitute for a real interview, but it helps you feel more confident.

## Step 1: Ask AI to be the interviewer

Copy this prompt and fill in the brackets:

> You are a hiring manager interviewing for a [Job Title] role at a [type of company, e.g., hospital, clinic]. Ask me one interview question at a time. Wait for my answer, then give me kind, honest feedback on what I said well and what I could improve. Then ask the next question. Start with "Tell me about yourself."

## Step 2: Answer out loud

Answer each question out loud, as if you were in the room. Then read the AI's feedback. If it suggests a better way to say something, try again.

## Step 3: Practice common questions

You can also ask AI for a list of common questions for your field:

> Give me 10 common interview questions for a [Job Title] role, with tips on how to answer each one in plain English.

Common questions include:

- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want this job?
- What is your greatest strength?
- What is a challenge you faced at work, and how did you handle it?
- Why should we hire you?

## Step 4: Practice your own questions

At the end of most interviews, they will ask if you have questions for them. Prepare 2–3. AI can help:

> I am interviewing for a [Job Title] role. Give me 3 good questions to ask the employer at the end of the interview.

Good questions are about the team, training, or what success looks like — not about pay or vacation in the first interview.

## Step 5: Record yourself (optional)

If your phone can record voice memos, record your answer and listen to it. You will notice things like speaking too fast or saying "um" a lot. This is normal — practice fixes it.

## A reminder

AI feedback is helpful, but it is not a hiring manager. If something the AI says does not feel right, trust your own judgment. And never paste your real name, address, or employer details into the chat.

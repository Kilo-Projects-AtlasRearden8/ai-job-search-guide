# Cheat Sheet — Ready-to-Copy Prompts

Copy and paste these prompts into your AI chat. Replace the bracketed parts with your own information. Remember: never paste your real address, Social Security number, or patient details.

## Resume

> You are a friendly career coach. Write a one-page resume in plain English. Use these details: [Name], [Email], [Phone], [Summary], [Experience], [Education], [Skills], [Licenses]. Use sections: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Licenses. Bullet points, one line each. Do not invent experience. Use [City, State] instead of a real address.

## Tailor a resume to a job

> Here is a job posting: [paste job description]. Here is my resume: [paste resume]. Rewrite my resume to highlight the skills this job asks for. One page. Plain English. Do not invent experience I do not have.

## Cover letter

> You are a friendly writing coach. Write a one-page cover letter in plain English. Job: [Job Title] at [Company]. My experience: [2–3 sentences]. Why I want this job: [1–2 sentences]. A work story: [2–3 sentences]. Opening, body, story, closing asking for an interview. Warm but professional. Do not sound robotic. Use [City, State] for address.

## Interview practice

> You are a hiring manager interviewing for a [Job Title] role. Ask me one question at a time. Wait for my answer, give kind feedback, then ask the next question. Start with "Tell me about yourself."

## Common interview questions

> Give me 10 common interview questions for a [Job Title] role, with tips on how to answer each one in plain English.

## Questions to ask the employer

> I am interviewing for a [Job Title] role. Give me 3 good questions to ask the employer at the end of the interview.

## Follow-up email

> Write a short, polite follow-up email for a job application. I applied for [Job Title] at [Company] on [Date]. I have not heard back. Friendly and under 100 words. Plain English.

## Application tracker

Use `site/tracker.html` in your browser. It saves in your browser only — nothing goes online.

## Safety reminder

Never paste: full address, Social Security number, full date of birth, bank numbers, passwords, patient details, or employer secrets. Use placeholders like [Your Name] and [City, State].
